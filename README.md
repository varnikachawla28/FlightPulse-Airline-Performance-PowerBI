#  FlightPulse – Airline Performance Dashboard

##  Project Overview

FlightPulse is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze airline performance across operational efficiency, financial performance, passenger experience, and executive-level business insights.

The project transforms airline data into an interactive Business Intelligence solution using Power BI, DAX, Power Query, data modeling, and data visualization.


##  Business Objectives

- Analyze overall airline performance
- Monitor flight and passenger activity
- Evaluate revenue, operating costs, and profitability
- Identify major contributors to flight delays
- Compare airline operational performance
- Analyze aircraft-level financial performance
- Evaluate passenger satisfaction and service quality
- Analyze booking channels and passenger demographics
- Identify business opportunities and areas for improvement
- Convert data into actionable business insights

##  Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level overview of airline performance through KPIs and visual analysis covering flights, passengers, revenue, profit, on-time performance, revenue trends, airline performance, and flight status.

### 2. Flight Operations

Analyzes operational performance through delay rate, average delay, delayed flights, delay reasons, delay trends, and airline operational performance.

### 3. Financial Performance

Analyzes revenue, operating costs, profitability, ticket pricing, aircraft profitability, aircraft category contribution, and the relationship between revenue and operating costs.

### 4. Passenger Experience

Analyzes passenger feedback and satisfaction using booking channels, passenger age groups, satisfaction distribution, service ratings, and customer satisfaction trends.

### 5. Executive Insights & Recommendations

Summarizes key business findings through airline profitability, delay reasons, profit margin, aircraft revenue performance, booking channels, and revenue versus operating cost analysis.

##  Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, airlines can focus on:

- Reducing operational delays and improving on-time performance
- Monitoring operating costs and improving cost efficiency
- Evaluating airline and aircraft profitability
- Improving passenger experience using satisfaction and service-rating insights
- Using booking-channel analysis to understand customer behavior
- Combining operational, financial, and customer metrics for better decision-making
